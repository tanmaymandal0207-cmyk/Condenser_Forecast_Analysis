# Project Commitments

What this study commits to, so the client can hold the findings to a clear standard.

## Objective

Explain the PC-86207 Swift / Swift Dzire condenser decline with evidence, and identify which fitment, product-code or distributor opportunities are worth validating.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Challan dispatch history, Apr 2009 – May 2019 | Claims about post-2019 demand before reconciliation |
| PC-86207 and related Swift condenser codes | Non-Swift fitments, except to separate multi-application codes |
| Distributor purchasing concentration | Naming or publishing distributor-level transactions |
| Vehicle cohorts as a demand indicator | Treating vehicle population as replacement volume |
| Indicative competitor price references | Firm pricing recommendations without dated, like-for-like quotes |

## Analytical commitments

1. Every figure ties back to a challan total or a named source.
2. Observations and hypotheses are labelled separately.
3. Multi-application codes are never counted as single-model demand.
4. Consistent financial years, units and code definitions across all outputs.
5. Assumptions, exclusions and gaps are written down, not left implicit.
6. Unresolved ambiguity is flagged before delivery, not after.

## Data-handling commitments

- Raw customer records, distributor transactions and proprietary prices stay out of this repository.
- Client name, brand and part-code prefix are anonymised in all published material.
- Original working drafts are kept unchanged; revisions are new versions, logged in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).
